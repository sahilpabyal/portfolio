
# TwinScan
## Barcode Validation & Operational Monitoring

### Overview

TwinScan is a personal software project designed to support barcode scanning validation and monitoring in warehouse operations.

The application focuses on improving data consistency by checking scanned information, identifying potential mismatches and providing operators with a structured view of scanning activities.

---

### The Problem

In logistics workflows, barcode scanning is a critical step in identifying products, handling units and operational movements.

Incorrect associations, duplicate scans or inconsistent information can affect data reliability and create additional work for operators.

TwinScan aims to help identify these situations through validation rules and operational monitoring.

---

### Key Features

- **Barcode scanning:** acquisition of barcode information through compatible devices.
- **Scan validation:** checks scanned data against defined rules.
- **Mismatch detection:** identification of potentially inconsistent matches.
- **Operational monitoring:** visibility into scanning activities and their results.
- **Data tracking:** structured recording of scan information.
- **Zebra compatibility:** designed with warehouse scanning terminals in mind.

---

### Operational Workflow

1. The operator scans a barcode using a compatible device.
2. The application receives and processes the scanned information.
3. Validation rules check the consistency of the data.
4. Potentially suspicious matches are highlighted.
5. Results are made available for operational review.
