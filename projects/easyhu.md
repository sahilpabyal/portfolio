
# EasyHU
## Handling Unit & SSCC Management

### Overview

EasyHU is a personal software project focused on the identification, scanning and management of Handling Units (HU) through SSCC barcode recognition.

The application is designed to support warehouse operations by making unit identification and data collection more structured, traceable and accessible.

It can be used through a computer or compatible mobile scanning devices, including Zebra terminals.

---

### The Problem

Handling Units and SSCC codes are essential elements in warehouse operations.

During activities such as goods handling, picking and logistics transitions, incorrect or inconsistent identification can lead to operational errors and data quality issues.

EasyHU aims to provide a structured workflow for scanning, identifying and validating handling units.

---

### Key Features

- **SSCC scanning:** acquisition of SSCC/UDC barcodes using compatible devices.
- **Operator identification:** operator access through a personal PIN.
- **Data validation:** checks to identify inconsistent or suspicious matches.
- **Live monitoring:** operational dashboard for monitoring scanning activities.
- **Excel export:** structured export of collected information for further analysis.
- **Device compatibility:** designed for use on computers and Zebra scanning terminals.
- **Activity tracking:** records relevant operations to support traceability.

---

### Operational Workflow

1. The operator accesses the application using a personal PIN.
2. The operator scans an SSCC barcode.
3. The application processes and validates the scanned information.
4. The result is recorded and made available in the operational dashboard.
5. Collected information can be exported for further analysis.
